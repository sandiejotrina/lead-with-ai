# Lead with AI

Free tools and lessons for anyone who builds or works with AI agents.

**The idea:** anyone can build an agent. Making several agents work together is a leadership job. Each lesson here goes with a video on the **Business Automation Made Simple** YouTube channel, and comes with a tool you can use right away. No coding and no sign-up.

By Sandie Dela Cruz, AuD, OD, MBA: clinician, AI builder, and leadership trainer.

---

## Start here

1. **Open the tools website:** `https://sandiejotrina.github.io/lead-with-ai/` *(this link works once GitHub Pages is turned on)*
2. **Pick the lesson** that matches the video you watched.
3. **Open its tool** and follow the steps in the lesson.

You can also browse the `lessons` folder above. Each lesson has the key ideas, the exercise, and step-by-step instructions for its tool.

## The four rules (Lesson 1)

1. **Every agent has a job description, including what it does not do.**
2. **Nobody grades their own work.**
3. **Trust is earned in stages.**
4. **Every correction becomes a rule.**

## Your privacy

The tools are free. You enter your email once to open them. Only your first name (optional) and email are sent, to Sandie's list. They're never sold or shared.

Subscribing to Sandie's weekly tips is optional. You get full access either way, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

What you build in a tool is never sent to a server. It stays saved in your own browser so you can come back to it.
