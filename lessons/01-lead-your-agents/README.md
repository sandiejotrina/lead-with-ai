# Lesson 1: Your agents need a boss

**Video:** My AI Agents Kept Failing. So I Gave Them a Boss.
**Tool:** [Agent Team Builder](../../tools/agent-team-builder/)
**Time:** 15 minutes

---

## The big idea

When AI agents step on each other (duplicate work, made-up facts, nobody accountable), the fix usually isn't a better prompt or a new tool. It's leadership: clear roles, clean handoffs, independent checks, and someone who decides.

Clinics figured this out a long time ago. The front desk schedules, the technician tests, the doctor decides, and a second person checks before anything reaches the patient. Agent teams need the same structure.

## The four rules

| Rule | What it means | In a clinic |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Every agent has a job description, including what it does not do | One job each, with stated limits, so roles don't overlap | The front desk schedules. It doesn't triage symptoms. |
| 2. Nobody grades their own work | A separate checker finds problems before you see the work | A second person checks the order before it reaches the patient |
| 3. Trust is earned in stages | Approve everything at first, then batches, then let low-risk work run on its own | A new tech is supervised until they've shown they're competent |
| 4. Every correction becomes a rule | When you reject something, the reason becomes a permanent instruction | A near miss becomes a protocol change |

## Use the tool: step by step

Open the **[Agent Team Builder](../../tools/agent-team-builder/)**.

### Step 1. Look at the example (2 minutes)
1. The tool opens with an example team for a weekly newsletter.
2. Look at the **org chart** on the right. It shows the order work moves in: two agents, a checker, then you.
3. Look at **The four rules** box. The example scores **4/4**.

### Step 2. Start your own team (1 minute)
1. Click **Start fresh**.
2. In **What is this team for?**, type the goal. For example: "Answer customer emails" or "Write my weekly report."

### Step 3. Write job descriptions (5 minutes)
1. For your first agent, fill in **Name**, **Does**, and **Does NOT**.
2. Click **+ Agent** to add more agents, in the order the work happens.
3. Watch for orange warnings. They tell you when an agent is missing its limits, or when two agents sound like they do the same job.

### Step 4. Add a checker (2 minutes)
1. Click **+ Checker** and add it after your agents.
2. Give it a short **pass/fail checklist**, one check per line. For example: "Every fact has a source."
3. Use the **↑ ↓** arrows to move steps into the right order.

### Step 5. Add your gate (2 minutes)
1. Click **+ Your approval gate** and put it after the checker.
2. Say **what you approve** and **how often**. For example: "Once a week, all drafts together."

### Step 6. Add your rules (2 minutes)
1. In **Rules from your corrections**, write one thing you've had to correct before, as a rule. For example: "Keep it under 400 words."
2. Choose your **trust level**.

### Step 7. Use it (1 minute)
1. Check that **The four rules** shows **4/4**. If not, each rule tells you what's missing.
2. Click **Copy brief**.
3. Paste the brief into Claude, ChatGPT, or whatever tool runs your agents.
