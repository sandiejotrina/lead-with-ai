# Lesson 2 · Onboard your agent like a new hire

**Video:** How Do You Actually Use AI Agents? (Think Like a Manager)
**Tool:** [New Agent Onboarding Card](https://sandiejotrina.github.io/lead-with-ai/tools/agent-onboarding-card/)

## The short answer

You don't use AI agents like apps. You onboard them like new hires.

Most people open an agent, type "do my marketing," and get something generic. A real new hire would do the same with that instruction. The agent isn't the problem. The onboarding is.

## The four steps

1. **Give it a job, and say what it doesn't do.** Two lines. The "does not" line is the one almost everybody skips, and it's what stops an agent from doing other people's work.
2. **Give it a training packet.** Show it two or three examples of your best work. Examples beat describing your style.
3. **Supervise the first week, then let go a little.** Check everything at first. Then check in batches. Let low-risk work run on its own only once it's earned it, and decide what always comes back to you.
4. **When it's wrong, say why, and make it a rule.** Save the reason as a rule the agent follows from now on, so you never fix the same mistake twice.

## Your homework

Pick one agent you already use. Write its job in two lines, what it does and what it doesn't do, and give it three examples of good work. Then post your two-line job description in the video's comments.

## How to use the Onboarding Card

1. Open the [New Agent Onboarding Card](https://sandiejotrina.github.io/lead-with-ai/tools/agent-onboarding-card/) and enter your email to open it. It's free.
2. It opens with an example (a newsletter writer). Click **Start fresh** to make your own, or edit the example.
3. Fill in the four steps for one agent. Your card updates as you type.
4. Check **Ready for day one?** Anything marked Missing tells you exactly what to add.
5. Click **Copy instructions** and paste them into your agent's instructions in Claude, ChatGPT, or the tool you use. Attach the three examples you listed.

Everything you type stays in your own browser.
